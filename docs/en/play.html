    Quick rules
    • First, everyone places two villages, each with a road (seats 1→4, then 4→1). You get one card for each land around your second village.
    • Each turn starts with a dice roll. Every village next to a land with that number gets 1 card, and every town gets 2.
    • Road = wood + brick, village = wood + brick + wool + grain, town = 2 grain + 3 ore, development card = wool + grain + ore.
    • On a 7, anyone with 8 or more cards discards half, and the roller moves the robber and takes one card from a player next to it.
    • You can trade 4 cards for 1 with the bank. A village on a harbor makes it 3:1 or 2:1.
    • Reach 10 points on your own turn to win. Village 1, town 2, longest road 2, largest army 2, point card 1.

    Circles on the board show where you can build now. Tap one to get a confirm button. The dots under each number show how often it rolls (6 and 8 are the most common).

    "Isle Routes" is an original name made for this research project. The source code of the AI and these pages is on GitHub.
