Learn Isle Routes with a strong AI

A four-player game where you gather wood, brick, wool, grain and ore on a hexagonal island and build roads, villages and towns to be the first to 10 points. On this site you can play against a strong research AI and learn where to settle early and how to finish the game.

  1. RulesA short guide for first-time players: the island and resources, the opening, the turn and scoring.
  2. Play the AIPlay a four-player game against three AIs. When unsure, see the move the AI recommends and its estimate of everyone's chances.
  3. By the NumbersFrom AI-vs-AI games: the first-seat advantage, how winners score, and how the opening relates to winning.

The AI on this site

"Normal" is a greedy AI that looks one move ahead with an evaluation function, plus an exact search that never misses a win available this turn. "Strong" adds a search (MCTS) that samples dice rolls and hidden cards many times to look ahead. The research log is in EXPERIMENTS.md (Japanese).

"Isle Routes" uses an original name and artwork made for this research, following the basic rules of the classic four-player resource-and-settling genre. Trading between players is not supported yet; you can trade with the bank and harbors only.
